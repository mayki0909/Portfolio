Before posting, the owner should: confirm the rental sauna is free over the holidays; check the five sauna prices and five accessory prices on the day; say whether accessories can arrive before Christmas; supply real photos (the rental sauna at an event, the team) where they have them.

Research for this campaign (18 Nov 2026): competitors (Onni and Tesarko rent mobile saunas in Kočevje; Onni from 90 € per 24 h; no one shows a rental refund on purchase), December hooks (Miklavž and Finland's Independence Day 6 Dec, UNESCO anniversary 17 Dec, solstice 21 Dec), and sauna advice that makes no health claims (Terme Krka's sauna guide, Finnish Sauna Society). Slovenian consumer law (ZVPot-1, Article 51) forbids claiming a product cures illness: keep competitors' "detox", "immunity" and similar out of every post.
